### **Phase 1: Understanding the Diffusion Policy's Data Requirements**

Before modifying any code, we must deeply understand the data needs of our target model. A diffusion policy learns to denoise a sequence of random actions into a coherent, goal-conditioned action plan. This has specific implications for the training data format.

**1. Data Modality: Delta Actions**
*   **Requirement**: The model must be trained on **delta actions**, representing the change in state from one timestep to the next. Our `ScriptedExpert` and `IKSolver` already generate these, but the dataset must be configured to exclusively save and yield this format.
*   **Action Space**: The delta action is an 8-dimensional vector: 7 for the arm's joint position changes and 1 for the gripper state. These actions must be consistently normalized to `[-1, 1]`.

**2. Data Structure: Observation and Action Horizons (Chunking)**
*   **Requirement**: Diffusion policies do not operate on single `(obs, action)` pairs. They require a **history of observations** to infer velocity and momentum, and they predict a **horizon of future actions** to produce smooth, non-myopic behavior.
*   **Observation Horizon (`history_len`)**: Each training sample must include a sequence of the `N` most recent observations.
    *   *Example*: `N=4` means each sample provides the observations from timesteps `t-3`, `t-2`, `t-1`, and `t`.
*   **Action Horizon (`future_len`)**: Each training sample must include the sequence of the `K` subsequent actions that the expert took.
    *   *Example*: `K=8` means each sample provides the ground-truth expert actions for timesteps `t`, `t+1`, ..., `t+7`.

**3. Final Sample Structure**
*   **Requirement**: Each sample yielded by the `ExpertDataset` should be a dictionary containing these chunks, ready for training.
*   **Proposed Structure**:
    ```
    {
        # Shape: (history_len, H, W, C)
        "image_primary_chunk": <torch.Tensor>,

        # Shape: (history_len, proprio_dim)
        "proprio_chunk": <torch.Tensor>,

        # Shape: (future_len, action_dim)
        "action_chunk": <torch.Tensor>
    }
    ```
    *This structured, chunk-based format is the single most important requirement.*

**4. Data Quality and Consistency**
*   **Requirement**: The dataset must consist of **complete and successful trajectories only**. A diffusion model trained on failed or incomplete attempts will learn suboptimal behaviors.
*   **Requirement**: The data must be generated with a consistent simulation `dt` and control frequency, as the magnitude of the delta actions is directly tied to this frequency.
