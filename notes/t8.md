This analysis defines the **Training Strategy** we will use later. It does not change the data *files* (download/LMDB) we just built, but it heavily dictates **how we use them** in the training loop.

Here is the breakdown of **APEX-MoE** (Adaptive Phase-locked Experts) in simple terms:

### 1. The Core Problem: "Cold Start Roulette"
If you train a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) from scratch (randomly initialized):
*   **Step 1:** The "Router" (the brain deciding who handles the patient) is stupid. It sends a patient dying of Sepsis to the "Healthy Expert" and a healthy patient to the "Sepsis Expert."
*   **Result:** The "Sepsis Expert" gets trained on healthy people. It loses its ability to detect rare critical events. The model becomes a generic average.

### 2. The Solution: APEX-MoE (Two-Stage Bootstrapping)
We separate the training into two distinct phases to guarantee specialization.

#### **Phase 1: The Generalist (Bootstrapping)**
*   **What we do:** Train a *single* `ICUForecaster` on **100% of the data** (Stable and Crash).
*   **Outcome:** This model becomes "Okay" at everything. It learns basic physiology (e.g., BP drops when HR rises) but might be too conservative on sudden crashes.
*   **The "Router" Creation:** We attach a small classifier (`SepsisHead`) to this trained model and freeze it. This is now our reliable "Router" that knows how to spot a crash.

#### **Phase 2: The Specialist (Phase-Locking)**
*   **What we do:** We **clone** the trained Generalist into two copies: `Expert_Stable` and `Expert_Crash`.
*   **The Trick:**
    *   `Expert_Stable`: Gets fine-tuned *only* when the Router says "Probability(Sepsis) < 10%".
    *   `Expert_Crash`: Gets fine-tuned *only* when the Router says "Probability(Sepsis) > 10%".
*   **Benefit:** The `Expert_Crash` *only* sees pure, high-risk data. It becomes "Hyper-Sensitive" to the warning signs that the Generalist ignored.

### 3. How this affects our Code
Since we just wrote `diffusion_icu_dataset.py`, we are perfectly set up for this.

*   **We included `outcome_label` in the dataset.**
    *   **Phase 1** will ignore this label and train on everything.
    *   **Phase 2** will use this label to **filter data** inside the DataLoader.

**You do not need to change the previous file.** The `ICUSotaDataset` is robust enough to support this strategy via its sampler logic, which we will implement in the **Training Script** phase.
